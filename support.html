Help

Log a win

  1. Open Winsprout in Teams (Apps › Winsprout).
  2. On Today, write what you got done, choose a type and a size (Small, Solid or Big).
  3. Press Add win or Enter. To log a past day, change the date next to the button.

Keep your wins safe on every device

Go to Settings › Back up to my OneDrive and sign in with your work account. Your wins are then saved in OneDrive › Apps › Winsprout and appear on all your devices. If your organisation requires it, an IT admin may need to approve Winsprout once.

Streaks and goals

Your streak counts working days in a row with at least one win. Days off (set in Settings) never break it. Your weekly goal is shown as a ring on Today.

Weekly summary

On My week, press Copy week summary and paste it in a Teams chat with your manager.

Appraisal report

On Year report, choose a period and press Save Word report to my OneDrive (or Download Word report), or Copy report to paste into an appraisal form.

Share a talent

  1. On Talents, pick your talents (or add your own).
  2. Under Share a talent moment, add a title and a few words, plus a photo or a link to your video or audio (at least one). Upload clips to your own OneDrive, Stream or YouTube first and check colleagues can open the link.
  3. Press Choose a chat or channel. The first time, Microsoft asks you to allow Winsprout to see chat and channel names and post messages you send. Some organisations need an IT admin to approve this. If so, press Copy request for IT in Winsprout and send it to your IT helpdesk, or share the IT admins page.
  4. Pick a chat or a team channel, check the preview, and press Post to Teams. You can also press Copy post and paste it yourself.

Posts appear under your own name. To remove one, delete it in Teams. Removing it from My talent moments only removes it from your list.

Daily reminder

In Settings › Daily reminder, choose a time and press Add reminder to my calendar. Open the file and save it in Outlook.

Common questions

Can my manager see my wins? No. Only you can, unless you share a summary or report yourself.

Can Winsprout read my Teams messages? No. It only sees the names of your chats, teams and channels so you can pick where to post, and it only posts what you write and confirm.

I changed computers and my wins are gone. Connect OneDrive backup in Settings on both devices. Without it, wins are saved only in the browser where you wrote them.
