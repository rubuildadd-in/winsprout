Winsprout by RuBuild Services

Remember everything you achieved this year.

Winsprout is a free, private wins journal inside Microsoft Teams. Log your wins in 30 seconds a day, keep a streak going, turn a year of small wins into a polished appraisal report, and share your talents with colleagues.

FreeInside Microsoft TeamsYour wins stay in your own OneDrive

30 seconds a day

Write what you got done, pick a type (delivered, fixed, helped someone, customer win, learned, idea) and mark the big ones.

  • Prompts help you remember
  • Forgot yesterday? Log it for any past day
  • Optional daily reminder in your Outlook calendar

Grow, earn badges, feel proud

Every win helps you grow from 🌰 Seed to 🏞️ Forest. A weekly goal, a streak that days off never break, and 18 badges with progress toward the next one.

  • Share a proud moment, a badge or your level with your manager or team in Teams
  • See your week in a clear chart and copy a summary for your 1:1

Your appraisal report

Choose a period (last 12 months, financial year April to March, calendar year or custom) and get:

  • Totals, highlights and a month-by-month list
  • A Word report saved to your OneDrive
  • Or copy it into any appraisal form

Share your talents

Storyteller, singer, speaker, artist? Add your talents and share a talent moment with your colleagues whenever you like, without waiting for an HR event.

  • Post a title and a few words, plus a photo or a link to your video or audio
  • Straight into a Teams chat or channel you choose, from your own account
  • Or copy the post and paste it anywhere

Private by design

Winsprout is for you, not for monitoring. Your wins are saved in the app on your device and, if you connect it, in a private folder in your own OneDrive (Apps › Winsprout). RuBuild Services never receives or stores them, and there are no ads. Talent posts go straight from your account to the Teams chat or channel you pick. Winsprout never reads your messages. You choose what to share and with whom. See the privacy policy.

Where it works

Microsoft Teams on Windows, Mac, the web, iPhone and Android, with a Microsoft 365 work or school account. OneDrive backup uses your Microsoft 365 sign-in.
